Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

eBay Listing Assistant is a privately operated application being developed to help its authorized user create and manage eBay listings. This policy describes its intended handling of personal information and application data.

1. Information We Process

The application may process:

  • Photographs uploaded for creating listings.
  • Item descriptions, conditions, categories, prices, and other listing details.
  • Authorized eBay seller account information.
  • OAuth credentials necessary to access permitted eBay services.
  • Information required to prepare, validate, and manage eBay listings.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to identify products, generate listing descriptions, recommend categories and prices, transfer item photographs, and prepare listings for eBay.

The application is designed to require explicit approval from the account holder before publishing any listing.

3. Third-Party Services

The application is designed to integrate with OpenAI ChatGPT for item identification and listing preparation, and with official eBay APIs for account authorization and listing operations.

Third-party services may process or retain information according to their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Security and Storage

The application will be designed to restrict account access to authorized users and to protect API credentials and authentication tokens through secure backend storage.

Uploaded photographs and listing information may be processed temporarily during listing creation. The application's specific storage, retention, and deletion practices will be documented before live operation.

5. Account Authorization

Access to eBay account information requires authorization from the account holder. The account holder may revoke granted permissions through applicable eBay account settings.

6. Information Sharing

Information may be transmitted to eBay, OpenAI, and necessary application service providers to perform authorized listing functions.

The application is not intended to sell personal information or use account data for unrelated advertising purposes.

7. Data Access and Deletion

The account holder may contact the application operator to ask about information retained by the application or to request deletion of applicable stored data.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as application functionality and data-handling practices are finalized or modified.
